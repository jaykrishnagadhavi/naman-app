# ABrainoConscious Email Subject Lines

Paste these recommended subject lines into the Supabase Dashboard under **Authentication -> Email Templates**.

| Template | Subject Line (Recommended) | Alternative Option |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Confirm Signup** | `Welcome to ABrainoConscious — Confirm your email` | `Confirm your student email to begin` |
| **Magic Link** | `Your quiet sign-in link for ABrainoConscious` | `Sign in to your learning sanctuary` |
| **Reset Password** | `Reset your ABrainoConscious password` | `Choose a new password for your account` |
| **Change Email Address** | `Confirm your new email address — ABrainoConscious` | `Verify email address update` |
| **Invite User** | `You're invited to join ABrainoConscious` | `Your invitation to Naman Studio` |

---

## Supabase Template Variables Reference
- `{{ .ConfirmationURL }}`: The action link generated by Supabase (contains token and redirect URL).
- `{{ .Email }}`: The recipient's email address.
- `{{ .SiteURL }}`: Your configured Supabase Site URL.
- `{{ .Token }}`: 6-digit verification code (if using OTP code in email body).
